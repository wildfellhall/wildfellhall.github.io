THE TENANT OF WILDFELL HALL — PRINTING REFERENCES

Anne Bronte / Acton Bell, The Tenant of Wildfell Hall, volume I.
London: T. C. Newby, 1848. Scan: Internet Archive, tenantofwildfell01bronrich.
https://archive.org/details/tenantofwildfell01bronrich
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_tenant_of_Wildfell_Hall_(IA_tenantofwildfell01bronrich).pdf
Studied printed pages 3–4 (scan pages 15–16): proportional Roman type,
justified paragraphs, first-line indents, consistent baselines, and locally
uneven dark impressions. The scan is a layout/impression reference, not a
colour calibration. The site's ivory rag paper and romantic borders remain.
The scan and its prose are not redistributed as website content.

IM FELL English Roman, Igino Marini.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/imfellenglish
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/imfellenglish/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html
https://iginomarini.com/fell/the-revival-fonts/
A freely licensed revival of the earlier Fell types, chosen for their
irregular cut serifs, uneven stroke weight and characterful book typography.
This is an intentionally older typographic interpretation, not a claim that
Newby's 1848 edition used this face. The 1848 pages guide the composition
and ink impression. The unmodified IMFeENrm28P.ttf is served locally.
SIL OFL 1.1: /ornaments/imfellenglish-OFL.txt. No paid font or runtime font
service is used.

Book ink has a separate procedural impression within the press renderer.
Continuous local ink-load fields change density inside each stroke; wet
patches spread slightly and raised paper fibres leave small gaps. Contours
vary below the letter scale while baselines and word spacing stay typeset.
The page seed and position give repeated letters different impressions;
turning back to a page restores the same ink. Carbon-black strokes retain
their dark centres instead of becoming uniformly translucent brown text.
Shallow compression and an ink-dependent roughness map couple the same
impression to the paper surface. Reduced surface sheen keeps the dark ink
visible under the existing room lighting. The font file is not modified.

Paragraph composition shares tex-linebreak and the British hyphenation
patterns documented in /gazette/references.txt. Discretionary hyphens are
rendering only; the editable book.txt and accessible text keep the author's
words. Blank paragraphs and explicit --- page breaks retain their meaning.
