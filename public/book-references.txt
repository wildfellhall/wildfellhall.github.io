THE TENANT OF WILDFELL HALL — PRINTING REFERENCES

Anne Bronte / Acton Bell, The Tenant of Wildfell Hall, volume I.
London: T. C. Newby, 1848. Scan: Internet Archive, tenantofwildfell01bronrich.
https://archive.org/details/tenantofwildfell01bronrich
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_tenant_of_Wildfell_Hall_(IA_tenantofwildfell01bronrich).pdf
Studied printed pages 3–4 (scan pages 15–16): proportional Roman type,
justified paragraphs, first-line indents, consistent baselines, and locally
uneven dark impressions. The scan is a layout/impression reference, not a
colour calibration. The site's ivory rag paper and romantic borders remain.
The scan and its prose are not redistributed as website content.

Old Standard TT Regular, Alexey Kryukov / The Old Standard Project Authors.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oldstandardtt
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/oldstandardtt/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html
A freely licensed revival of Modern-style type associated with later
nineteenth- and early twentieth-century books; a visual interpretation,
not an identification or exact reconstruction of Newby's 1848 printing type.
The unmodified OldStandard-Regular.ttf is served locally. SIL OFL 1.1:
/ornaments/old-standard-tt-OFL.txt. No paid font or runtime font service is used.

Book ink shares the Gazette's procedural press treatment, with fewer fibre
voids and lighter local drag to preserve fine serif hairlines. Small ink
squeeze, shallow paper compression and an ink-dependent roughness map keep
the impression in the paper under the existing room lighting. The font file
is not distressed or modified: variation is applied to each printed page.

Paragraph composition shares tex-linebreak and the British hyphenation
patterns documented in /gazette/references.txt. Discretionary hyphens are
rendering only; the editable book.txt and accessible text keep the author's
words. Blank paragraphs and explicit --- page breaks retain their meaning.
