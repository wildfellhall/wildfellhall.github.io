The revised Gazette also uses contents leaders and numbered continuations.

The Gazette blends these visual traditions; it is not a facsimile of either
publication. Publication dates are real contemporary dates. Its house and
botanical vignette are original code-drawn linework; reference scans are not
redistributed as website assets. Paper, ink wear, folds and twine are procedural.
Empty editorial sections contain no invented articles or imitation body text.

Typefaces (modern revivals, locally bundled):
UnifrakturMaguntia — ornamental blackletter masthead.
Old Standard TT — newspaper body, italic decks, and bold serif headlines;
a revival of the Modern/classicist printing types of the later 19th century.
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/oldstandardtt/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html
Libre Baskerville remains used for the book's gold-leaf title.
TT2020 Style E — the book's varied typewriter impressions.
Their SIL Open Font License files are included in /ornaments/.
