WILDFELL GAZETTE — DESIGN REFERENCES

The Illustrated London News, first issue, 14 May 1842.
Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs, item 95514551.
https://www.loc.gov/item/95514551/
Reference scan viewed:
https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/pnp/cph/3c10000/3c15000/3c15100/3c15113v.jpg
Studied: decorated masthead, engraved architectural vignette, horizontal
date rules, narrow columns, small serif headlines, and wood-engraving hatching.

The Leader, 4 January 1851, page 1.
Nineteenth-Century Serials Edition (ncse), a scholarly digital edition
in collaboration with the British Library, Birkbeck and King's College London.
https://ncse.ac.uk/periodicals/l/issues/cld_04011851/page/1/
Reference scan viewed:
https://ncse.ac.uk/static/media/periodicals/101-CLD-1851-01-04-001-SINGLE/Pg001_W3pt8Xy.png
Studied: blackletter masthead with flourishes, dated issue line, compact
article headings, three-column composition and uneven letterpress impressions.

The Leader, 4 January 1851, page 2.
https://ncse.ac.uk/periodicals/l/issues/cld_04011851/page/2/
Reference scan viewed:
https://ncse.ac.uk/static/media/periodicals/101-CLD-1851-01-04-001-SINGLE/Pg002_8Ddiqj0.png
Studied: running title and folio, justified narrow columns, contrasting
headlines and subheads, printer's rules and uneven oil-based ink impressions.
The revised Gazette also uses contents leaders and numbered continuations.

The Gazette blends these visual traditions; it is not a facsimile of either
publication. Publication dates are real contemporary dates. Its house and
botanical vignette are original code-drawn linework; reference scans are not
redistributed as website assets. Paper, ink wear, folds and twine are procedural.
Empty editorial sections contain no invented articles or imitation body text.

Typefaces (freely licensed modern revivals, locally bundled):
IM FELL DW Pica — article text and real italic decks/bylines.
IM FELL Great Primer — compact Roman headings and running titles.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/imfelldwpica
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/imfellgreatprimer
These originate in seventeenth-century types. Fell types were revived in
nineteenth-century book publishing; their use here is an interpretation of
printed character, not a claim that these were standard gazette faces.
https://www.c82.net/printing-types/chapters/21

Besley, Owen Earl — restrained Clarendon-style notices beneath the masthead.
A modern interpretation, with changes for contemporary text readability.
https://indestructibletype.com/Besley.html

Chomsky, Fredrick Brennan — blackletter outlines for the original Gazette
wordmark composition, with code-drawn pen flourishes. Its source is an
American New York Times-inspired face, not an exact British revival.
https://github.com/ctrlcctrlv/chomsky
The wordmark is regenerated by scripts/make-gazette-masthead.mjs.
The four SIL Open Font License notices are included in /ornaments/.
Libre Baskerville and TT2020 Style E remain used for the book.

Ink/material reference:
University of Illinois Preservation Self-Assessment Program, Carbon Black Ink.
https://psap.library.illinois.edu/collection-id-guide/inkothermedia
Dark carbon ink, restrained edge spreading, fine paper-fibre interruptions.
Monochrome scans inform composition and impression, not exact RGB values.
The newspaper has its own fine cream stock, independent ink roughness and
subtle impression depth. Most ink coverage remains opaque. The room's
existing lighting shades the paper and printing together.

Composition:
tex-linebreak, Robert Knight (MIT), with its bundled Hypher implementation
by Bram Stein (BSD). Paragraph-level line breaking and British English
hyphenation keep narrow columns compact. Hyphens affect rendering only.
https://github.com/robertknight/tex-linebreak
British patterns: Dominik Wujastyk and Graham Toal, hyph-utf8 (MIT).
https://github.com/hyphenation/tex-hyphen/blob/master/hyph-utf8/tex/generic/hyph-utf8/patterns/tex/hyph-en-gb.tex
The unmodified pattern source and license notices are in /licenses/.
The source converts to src/gazette-british-patterns.js with
scripts/make-gazette-patterns.mjs. No dictionary or service is fetched at runtime.

Archive frame:
The same modeled brass moulding, beads, acanthus and scrollwork as the desk
controls, rebuilt for the Archive's proportions with a recessed linen backing.
Day/evening stills are rendered from that geometry for non-WebGL browsers.
