Wildfell Hall

Scroll, pinch, or use the plus and minus keys to zoom. Drag or use the arrow keys to inspect the desk. Press Home or use Reset view to return to the starting view. Click or tap a drawer to open or close it, or Tab to its knob and press Enter or Space. Click the maroon book to open it. Double-click or double-tap the book for a full-screen reading view, or use the Read book full screen button. Click the right page to turn forward, or the left page to turn back. With the book open, the left and right arrow keys turn pages. Escape returns from reading view to the same page on the desk; otherwise it closes the book. Tab also reaches the book and page controls. Click the loosely folded newspaper to unfold the Wildfell Gazette on the desk. Its reading control opens a separate reading view. The calendar opens a date list; the Gazette arrows turn continuation sheets. Escape returns to the desk.
