# Project requirements

- Do not use the Sites or image generation skills or services at any point in this project. This is an explicit user instruction.
- Build and maintain the website directly in this repository. Keep it compatible with static GitHub Pages hosting.
- The current scope is the antique white wooden writing desk, its atmosphere, a small maroon leather-bound copy of The Tenant of Wildfell Hall near the corner, and the Wildfell Gazette. Keep the book's aged, turnable pages, romantic printed borders, editable text in public/book.txt, and full-screen reading view. The Gazette is a bundle of separately shaded, loosely folded leaves with untied twine beside it; one click unfolds it on the desk, a separate control opens reading view, and a simple date list browses issues. Use real dates starting 29 October 2026. Keep its three articles, Thought a Day, Words of the Week and Miscellany empty until supplied. Dated issue files and the reusable template live in public/gazette/; the archive index is generated at build time. Other books, navigation, and unrelated content are future work.
- Use procedural materials and actual geometry for the desk. Preserve the quiet Victorian/Romantic-inspired character and the clear writing surface.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences, touch input, and the non-WebGL fallback.
- Keep the realistic ivory wood grain behind the book in full-screen reading mode, using the same desktop material and room lighting.
- Visible lettering is limited to the crest, gold-leaf book title, the user's book text, the Gazette's masthead and editorial sections, and its date archive. Use the locally served IM FELL English face in the book, with visibly uneven local ink coverage and worn edges. Keep the distinction between its earlier type design and the 1848 layout/impression references documented in public/book-references.txt. Keep justified paragraphs, modest first-line indents, paper-dependent letterpress ink, and romantic scroll borders. Preserve the user’s text and explicit page breaks, and verify the actual fonts and rendered pages on the live site. Use a minimum four-page Gazette edition with automatic article continuations, a contents strip, running heads, folios, narrow justified columns, printer’s rules, and optional editable subtitle decks. Use distressed letterpress-style serif text and an ornamental masthead for the Gazette, guided by the authentic scans documented in public/gazette/references.txt. Keep unsupplied prose blank. Do not add unrelated headings, captions, monograms, or visible control labels over the desk.
- Preserve the intricate ivory relief carvings and the ornate light pink carpet beneath the desk.
