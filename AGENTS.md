# Project requirements

- Do not use the Sites or image generation skills or services at any point in this project. This is an explicit user instruction.
- Build and maintain the website directly in this repository. Keep it compatible with static GitHub Pages hosting.
- The current scope is the antique white wooden writing desk, its atmosphere, and a small maroon leather-bound copy of The Tenant of Wildfell Hall near the corner, with aged, turnable pages, romantic printed borders, editable text in public/book.txt, and a full-screen reading view. Other papers, books, navigation, and personal/project content are future work.
- Use procedural materials and actual geometry for the desk. Preserve the quiet Victorian/Romantic-inspired character and the clear writing surface.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences, touch input, and the non-WebGL fallback.
- Keep the realistic ivory wood grain behind the book in full-screen reading mode, using the same desktop material and room lighting.
- Visible lettering is limited to the gold cursive “Wildfell Hall” on the desktop, the gold-leaf book title “The Tenant of Wildfell Hall”, and the user's book text from public/book.txt. Typeset the pages in a period-inspired book serif with ornate romantic scroll borders. Keep them blank until text is supplied. Do not add headings, captions, monograms, or visible control labels over the desk.
- Preserve the intricate ivory relief carvings and the ornate light pink carpet beneath the desk.
